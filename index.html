read this first — what this is and isn't

ICASSP's full name is "Acoustics, Speech, and Signal Processing" — it is a broad conference, and speech is one slice of it. Right now only titles + DOIs are public for all papers (abstracts exist for just 17% via Semantic Scholar; IEEE gates the rest). So this is a titles-only theme map — a reliable read of what topics are present and how much, but not the per-paper problem/approach/contribution analysis that needs abstracts. That deeper pass waits for open indexing. Interspeech 2026 (Sydney, Sept 28–Oct 1) isn't published yet and joins later.

Why "Other" is so large, honestly

The single biggest bucket is a catch-all — ICASSP has a long tail of signal-processing subfields (estimation, optimization, arrays, statistical SP, specialized applications) that no small keyword set cleanly names from a title. That's a real property of the venue, not a bug in the map; a deeper pass with abstracts would split it further.

How the speech work is being done

Independent of topic, these method-signatures show up across the speech papers — the current toolkit:

efficiency / on-device72 · 12.2%
adversarial / robust / privacy49 · 8.3%
LLM-based32 · 5.4%

Counts are of the 590 speech/audio papers whose title carries each signature. Efficiency/on-device and adversarial/robustness lead — the field is optimizing and hardening, alongside the newer diffusion- and LLM-based waves.

The one-line read

ICASSP 2026 is first a signal-processing conference and only second a speech one — but within its speech slice, the story is recognition and enhancement as the steady core, with self-supervised foundations, anti-spoofing, and a fresh diffusion/LLM wave rising on top.

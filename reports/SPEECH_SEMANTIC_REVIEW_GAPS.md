## Review policy

Do not resolve ambiguity by selecting the paper's most fashionable model name. Resolve it by identifying the ordinary problem, the information changed by the method, the intended target, and the evidence depth. If the abstract does not distinguish those, keep the row ambiguous or insufficient and send it to full-paper review.
